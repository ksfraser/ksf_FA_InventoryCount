# BR-IC-003 — Location Decommissioning Transfers

**Module:** ksf_FA_InventoryCount

## Business Need

Temporary selling locations (e.g. trade-fair shops) hold inventory for a
season, then must be emptied back into permanent stores. Counting every item
at both ends is wasteful when the whole location is being moved anyway.

## Business Requirement

The business requires a one-step operation that transfers ALL stocked items
from one location to another (including the HOLDING tank), assuming a full
count will follow later.
