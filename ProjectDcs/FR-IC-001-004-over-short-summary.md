# FR-IC — Over/short summary

**Module:** ksf_FA_InventoryCount
**Parent:** BR-IC-001 / BR-IC-003 / BR-IC-004 as applicable

## Functional Requirement

The system SHALL compute, per line, the variance between counted quantity and system QOH at the selected location/date, and classify lines as matched / over / short with aggregate quantities.
